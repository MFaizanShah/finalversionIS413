What the file needs

  • One row per student, one column per assessment, and a final grade or final mark column.
  • Any layout works: the columns are detected and you check them on the next screen. Missing weights, maximum marks or student IDs are worked out from the file and shown to you, never a reason to reject it.
  • A column that is only the total of other columns (such as continuous assessment) is detected and not counted twice.
  • After you save, the model retrains in the background{% if not training_enabled %} (switched off on this copy){% endif %}.

Privacy: student IDs are replaced with codes when you save. Names and other personal columns are never stored.
